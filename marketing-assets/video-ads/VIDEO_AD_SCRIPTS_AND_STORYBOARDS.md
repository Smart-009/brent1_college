## 🛠️ How to Generate & Record the Video Advert

1. Open the file **`marketing-assets/video-ads/video_ad_generator.html`** in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Brave).
2. Choose your **Aspect Ratio** (9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 1:1 for Feed, 16:9 for YouTube).
3. Select any **Campaign Preset** (or edit the text directly in the control panel).
4. Click **`🎬 Record & Export Video (.WebM/MP4)`**.
5. The studio will record the smooth 60 FPS animated motion graphic and automatically download the completed video file straight to your computer or phone!
